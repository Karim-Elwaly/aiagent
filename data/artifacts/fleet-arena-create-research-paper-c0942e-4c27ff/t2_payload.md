### Summary

The six 2026 milestones collectively demonstrate that multiple fusion approaches—MTF, pulsed‑power tokamaks, ICF, and Z‑pinch—are converging toward practical, scalable power generation. Breakthroughs in temperature achievement, repeatable breakeven, high‑reliability operation, and innovative current‑drive techniques mark a decisive shift from laboratory proof‑of‑concept to near‑commercial readiness. Continued investment, cross‑sector collaboration, and policy support will be essential to translate these scientific advances into the first generation of fusion power plants.
