**Takeaway**

2026 was a pivotal year for the ESP‑32 family: new silicon (P4 v3.x, S31), major SDK evolution with ESP‑IDF v6.0 and ESP‑Matter “latest”, and strategic partnerships that broadened the ecosystem into automotive, industrial IoT, and consumer AIoT markets. These milestones collectively expanded performance, security, and connectivity options while accelerating time‑to‑market for Matter‑enabled devices and high‑throughput edge solutions.
